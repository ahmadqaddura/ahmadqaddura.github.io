About

Ahmad Qaddura (b. 1988) is a Palestinian–Swedish editorial cartoonist based in Gothenburg, Sweden. His work examines international affairs, social realities, and human rights.

He is a member of Cartooning for Peace, with cartoons published in Dagens Nyheter and syndicated internationally through Cartoon Movement and CartoonStock.
