About the Artist

Ahmad Qaddura is a Gothenburg-based editorial cartoonist and satirical illustrator whose work bridges classic ink-and-engraving traditions with direct, minimalist visual commentary.

Drawing inspiration from timeless political caricature, his illustrations examine international affairs, human dignity, and everyday economic realities—translating complex headlines into immediate, thought-provoking visual statements.

His editorial cartoons have been published in major Swedish press including Dagens Nyheter, featured in the satirical anthology book Tidötiden (2026), and syndicated internationally through CartoonStock.
