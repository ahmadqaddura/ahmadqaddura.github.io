About the Artist

Ahmad Qaddura is a Gothenburg-based editorial cartoonist and satirical illustrator whose work bridges classic ink-and-engraving traditions with direct, minimalist visual storytelling.

Drawing inspiration from timeless political caricature, his illustrations examine international affairs, human dignity, and everyday economic realities—translating complex headlines into immediate visual statements.

His cartoons have appeared in major Swedish press such as Dagens Nyheter, the satirical anthology Tidötiden, and international syndication platforms.
